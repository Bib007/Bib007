## Your territory cards. One clear overview.

**For people managing territories in congregations of Jehovah's Witnesses.**

Run LTM on your own **Mac, Windows computer or Home Assistant**. Start with paper cards and assignment records, then add addresses and map drawing at your own pace.

**[Website](https://localterritorymanager.netlify.app/) · [Try the demo](https://bib007.github.io/LTM-Local-Territory-Manager/) · [Installation guide](https://github.com/Bib007/LTM-Local-Territory-Manager/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.en.md)**

*The demo needs no account or installation and uses fictional examples. Explore administrator and territory-desk assistant views.*

[![LTM dashboard with fictional records](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Bib007/LTM-Local-Territory-Manager/main/assets/screenshots/dashboard-en.jpg)](https://bib007.github.io/LTM-Local-Territory-Manager/)

- **Use your own spreadsheets.** Import supported card and IB records from Excel or CSV, choose the sheet and columns, review the preview and confirm.
- **Work together.** Individual accounts, custom roles and a guided introduction tailored to each user's permissions. Review conflicting edits while keeping the saved proposals.
- **See useful reports.** Calendar-year, service-year and rolling-12-month summaries, including territories without a recorded completion in the last 12 months.
- **Print when needed.** Territory cards, IB backs, S-13 and summaries.

English is the default for new users; Danish and saved language choices remain available. Translation is partial, and original printed forms remain Danish. Records stay on your installation; map backgrounds, address lookups and optional public image sharing use external services. Address tools are primarily designed for Denmark.

<summary><strong>Dansk · Om LTM</strong></summary>

LTM samler distriktskort, udleveringer, afleveringer og historik på din egen computer eller Home Assistant. Behold papirkortene, importér dine egne Excel-/CSV-filer med valg af kolonner, og tilføj adresser og korttegning efter behov.

Individuelle brugere får egne rettigheder og en tilpasset guidet introduktion. Demoen har en administratorvisning og en ind-/udleveringsassistent og bruger kun opdigtede oplysninger.

**[Hjemmeside på dansk](https://localterritorymanager.netlify.app/da/) · [Dansk installationsguide](https://github.com/Bib007/LTM-Local-Territory-Manager/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md)**

Engelsk er standardsproget for nye brugere; dansk og gemte sprogvalg bevares. Oversættelsen er delvis, og de oprindelige printformularer er fortsat danske. Kortbaggrunde, adresseopslag og valgfri offentlig billeddeling bruger eksterne tjenester.
